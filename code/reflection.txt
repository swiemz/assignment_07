One thing this assignment made me realize is how much the structure of a webpage determines the way I collect data.
In this assignment, the three coffee shop pages all lived in different sections, and I had to use a variety
of techniques to scrape the text. For example, I used CSS selectors for Lakeshore Lattes, read_html() and melt() 
functions for Hilltop Grind, and I had to manually look up item prices as they were buried in the Canal Street coffee
webpage. I found this assignment very challenging, particularly in step 7, when writing the match_items function. It 
was difficult because I had to keep track of both how similar two names were and what the owner had already decided. 
It was overall a very tedious assignment, and I relied on AI tools to vibe code a few of the sections. While I understand the
overall structure of the assignment, I found it extremeley difficult to write a code that made sense. When I eventually did finish 
the sections, I didn't run into trouble with the tests. In the future, I would like to spend more time
working with scraper functions, because I do think it is an interesting and relevant technique. However, I need to build more
confidence around the codign aspect itself, instead of relying heavily on AI.
